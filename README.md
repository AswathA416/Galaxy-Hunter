
# GALAXY FALL

A third-person galaxy battle royale that runs in your browser. One file, no installs, no accounts. Cost: $0.

## Controls
- **Move:** WASD
- **Jump / open glider:** Space
- **Sprint:** Shift
- **Look:** mouse (click the game to capture it, Esc to release)
- **Fire:** left click
- **Open chest:** E
- **Weapons:** 1 pickaxe, 2–4 guns, mouse wheel cycles
- **Build mode:** Q, then 1 wall, 2 floor, 3 ramp

## Play
Open the GitHub Pages link for this repo, or download `index.html` and double-click it.

## Limits
- Building is wall, floor and ramp only.
- Bots are AI opponents, not other players.
